# Commit convention

## Format

Every new commit in Visual AI Studio must follow this format:

    <Porcupine Tree song title> - <what was done>

The first part must be the title of a Porcupine Tree song.
Only the song title is used, never lyrics.

The second part must briefly and clearly describe the change made by the commit.

## Examples

    Trains - extraction du projet autonome Visual AI Studio

    Lazarus - correction de l'import des livrables

    Fear of a Blank Planet - amélioration de la gestion des secrets

## Scope

This convention applies to all new commits made after the standalone repository migration.

Historical commits imported from the former H9 repository are preserved unchanged.
